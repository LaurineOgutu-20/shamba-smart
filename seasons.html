PREVENTION

Disease Prevention Practices

  • Use healthy planting material.
  • Inspect crops regularly.
  • Maintain good field hygiene.
  • Avoid unnecessary waterlogging.
  • Practice crop rotation where appropriate.
  • Seek professional agricultural advice when disease identification is uncertain.
